Software engineer with 15+ years across enterprise systems, web products and interactive media. I have shipped facial-tracking and document-capture systems for Hong Kong public services, built property portals and CRMs, and started my career making educational games in C++ and DirectX. I care about systems that are reliable underneath and delightful on the surface.

I started out drawing game characters and writing C++ for educational titles, moved through Flash, PHP and property platforms, then spent seven years building document-automation and identity systems for Hong Kong government and enterprise clients. Today I'm based in London, UK, working across product engineering, 3D and AI.
