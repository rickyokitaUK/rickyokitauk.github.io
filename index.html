About me

I am a software engineer and active game developer, based in Liverpool, UK with roots in Hong Kong.

Full-stack software engineer with 15+ years of commercial experience building scalable web apps, SaaS backend services and IoT systems for marketplaces, government, real estate, fintech, retail, gaming and education. I own complete development cycles — from backend APIs and database design to frontend UX — and care about clean, maintainable, well-tested code. Alongside that, I am focusing more deeply on game development: gameplay systems, simulation games, horror experiences and AI-assisted production workflows in Unity.

I started by drawing game characters and programming educational titles in C++ and DirectX, then spent more than a decade shipping web, identity and document-automation systems. Today I apply that engineering foundation to gameplay systems, simulation projects and AI-assisted production in Unity.
