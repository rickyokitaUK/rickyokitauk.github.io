My approach

Software doesn’t have to feel like software.

I use game-system thinking, visual feedback and interactive 3D to make complex operations easier to understand. The result is practical software that communicates through action, progress and exploration.

01 · Clarity

Make complexity visible

Turn workflows, data and system states into experiences people can understand at a glance.

02 · Interaction

Design meaningful feedback

Use clear goals, responsive feedback and game design principles to make every action feel purposeful.

03 · Delivery

Build working products

Take ideas from prototype to production across web platforms, Unity and real-time Three.js experiences.

Why I keep building

More than twenty years of ideas, still in motion.

I started experimenting with game development in 2003, while I was still at secondary school. More than twenty years later, I am still building because the possibilities have never stopped feeling valuable.

I believe game design can move beyond entertainment. Software can invite curiosity, complex operations can become understandable systems, and people can express their stories through interactive worlds.

Used thoughtfully, AI can shorten the distance between imagination and a working prototype. My aim is to combine engineering discipline, game-system thinking and AI-assisted creation so more ideas can become experiences that others can explore.

Keep learning. Keep building. Keep ideas playable.
