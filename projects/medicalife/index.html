MedicaLife

A hospital-management simulation combining hospital operations, patient flow, diagnosis and treatment with progression and finance systems.

Overview

MedicaLife explores how a detailed hospital simulation can remain immediately readable. Rooms, staff and patient behaviour form the visible layer, while diagnosis, treatment, reputation, equipment and finance create the management loop underneath.

Design note

What classic hospital games get right

Classic hospital-management games stay readable because rooms, queues and patient needs are legible at a glance. MedicaLife keeps that clarity while exploring more modern diagnosis, progression and simulation systems underneath.

Architecture

The patient loop

Every patient walks the same pipeline; the hospital the player builds decides how fast and how safely they get through it.

  1. 01

    Arrival

    Patients spawn with a condition from the disease data

  2. 02

    Reception

    Check-in and routing

  3. 03

    Diagnosis

    GP, X-ray, diagnostics or psychiatry

  4. 04

    Treatment

    Room and staff matched to the diagnosis

  5. 05

    Outcome

    Cured, or lost to queues, walking or incidents

  6. 06

    Reputation & cash

    Feeds back into who arrives next

Pressures on every step

Staff availability (receptionist, doctor, nurse, janitor, security)Room capacity and layout distanceQueue lengthCleanliness and infectionIncidentsDisease and treatment data

Simulation & balancing

Testing the economy with automated agents

MedicaLife runs scripted AI players through the same scenario dozens of times, each with a different strategy, and reports survival, cash, reputation and win rate, so balancing problems show up before manual playtesting.

  1. 01

    Simulate

    7 strategy agents × 50 runs each

  2. 02

    Measure

    Win rate, survival days, cash, reputation, failure causes

  3. 03

    Audit

    Win rate 30–70%, ≥ 3 strategies, no dominant strategy, fun score ≥ 70

  4. 04

    Adjust

    Rules, prices and the value of rooms and staff

What I built

  • ●Designed the hospital building and room placement flow
  • ●Modelled patient states from arrival through diagnosis and treatment
  • ●Prototyped management UI for staffing, equipment and finance
  • ●Established a data-driven structure for adding diseases and treatments

Key challenges

  • ●Showing several simulation states without overwhelming the player
  • ●Keeping patient queues, room capacity and staff schedules predictable
  • ●Making management decisions readable at both room and hospital scale

Next steps

  • ●Stress-test patient flow with larger hospitals
  • ●Add staff specialisations and progression
